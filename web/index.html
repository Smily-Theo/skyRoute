About this prototype

SkyRoute Intelligence is a hackathon concept for exploring road journeys, hypothetical eVTOL mobility, and infrastructure planning across Tamil Nadu.

What the data means
  • Locations are illustrative geographic reference points.
  • Road distance, time, and cost are estimated—not live navigation.
  • All eVTOL stations, links, fares, and schedules are hypothetical.
  • Candidate scores are not regulatory, engineering, or safety assessments.
